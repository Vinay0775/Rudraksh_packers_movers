CUSTOMER TRUST & DATA PRIVACY

Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

Effective Date: September 2026 • Rudraksha Packers & Movers, Jaipur (Rajasthan)

1. Information We Collect

When you use Rudraksha Packers & Movers or our Express Parcel delivery service, we collect essential information required to fulfill your relocation and transport service:

  • Contact Information: Customer name, primary mobile number, alternate phone number, and pickup/delivery contact persons.
  • Location Data: Pickup address, destination address, and GPS coordinates to accurately calculate distance, provide driver navigation, and dispatch moving vehicles.
  • Inventory Details: List of items (furniture, electronics, appliances, cartons) provided through our live cost calculator or pre-move survey form.
  • Verification Credentials: Mobile OTP verification codes and Secure Handover PINs generated to protect goods during transit.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is strictly utilized for logistics operations, including:

  • Calculating fair, transparent relocation tariffs and road distance via GPS mapping.
  • Assigning verified moving staff and drivers to your pickup location on your scheduled moving date.
  • Enabling real-time tracking so you can monitor the movement of your moving truck or delivery bike.
  • Sending essential service notifications, booking invoices, and customer support updates via WhatsApp or SMS.

3. Data Protection & Security Handover

We enforce strict security practices to keep your personal information confidential:

  • We never sell, rent, or trade customer contact details to third-party telemarketers or external lead brokers.
  • All admin sessions and dispatch controls are protected with encrypted HMAC authentication tokens.
  • Delivery OTPs ensure that goods and parcels are only handed over to verified recipients authorized by the sender.

4. Cancellation & Refund Policy

We believe in fair customer policies:

  • Free Rescheduling: You can reschedule your relocation date at zero penalty up to 24 hours before the booked dispatch time.
  • Cancellation: If you cancel before our team is dispatched, any advance deposit is refunded promptly within 2-4 business days.
  • Transit Care Guarantee: All goods are packed with multi-layer bubble wrap, edge guards, and corrugated sheets to prevent handling damage.
